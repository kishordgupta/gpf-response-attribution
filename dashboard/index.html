Read the experiment correctly

Input and collection artifacts

Only cleaned response text enters the classifier. Metadata defines labels and splits; the prompt, article, identity label, model name, and company fields are not features. The known leading Gemini said collection wrapper is removed: it occurs in 386 Gemini web responses and no other model stream. Twelve generic response wrappers are also removed. Literal model/company masking changes no cleaned responses, so it is reported as a no-op.

Full and screened populations

The full corpus contains 7,056 responses. Screening conservatively drops all twelve model rows at five cells with known content/target inconsistencies, leaving 6,996 responses across 583 cells. Raw responses are not relabeled or repaired.

What remains confounded

Model labels also differ in platform, date, route, and potentially hidden settings. There are only two articles and one recorded response per condition. Some discriminative cues may be capture formatting or prompt-specific habits, not stable properties of model weights.

Prediction scores

SVM decision margins are ranking scores, not calibrated probabilities. A closed-set classifier must pick from known labels even when a new response came from another source. The final all-data fit is for demonstration; evaluation uses separate held-out predictions.

Metrics and reproducibility

Accuracy is the fraction of correct labels. Balanced accuracy averages per-class recall. Macro F1 averages per-class F1. Company classes have unequal sizes because the panel contains different numbers of model labels per company. Compare to the measured dummy baseline, not a universal “chance” number.

Vocabulary, IDF, scalers, and classifiers are fit inside each training fold. Consult the saved split manifests and audit files for group separation and normalized-duplicate purging. Hyperparameters are fixed for these descriptive comparisons; the best row is not an independently confirmed model-selection result.
